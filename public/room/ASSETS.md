## NYC window
- Photograph: “New York City skyline of Midtown East from across the East River” by James D. Forrester / Jdforrester, 2 September 2024.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:New_York_City_skyline_of_Midtown_East_from_across_the_East_River.jpg
- License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original photograph stored at `window/nyc.jpg`. Displayed as a wrapping panorama and cropped onto independent solid building facades, with time-dependent tint, parapets, rooftop equipment, and exterior window trim.
- Solar approximation: NOAA General Solar Position Calculations, https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF . Uses NYC latitude/longitude and UTC; does not simulate live weather.

## Modeled city and apartment hallway (current)
- The window no longer renders the skyline photograph. It uses Kenney City Kit (Commercial) 2.1 GLB buildings, shared colormap, modeled streets/sidewalks, and a sky dome.
- Model source: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial — CC0. License included at `city/License.txt`.
- The streetscape is NYC-inspired, not a geographically exact Manhattan reconstruction.
- Hallway design references: https://www.eca.nyc/work/715-park and https://aline-studio.com/upper-west-side-cooperative-hallway-redesign/ (neutral upper walls, lower paneling, crown/base moldings, sconces and apartment hardware). No reference photographs are embedded in the hallway.
